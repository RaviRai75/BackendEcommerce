# Revision-fenced abandoned-cart activity episodes

Task 36 records a private, best-effort projection of authenticated cart activity and closes the current episode when the cart is cleared or exactly converted into an order. A Cart-owned monotonic revision and an owner-unique projection row serialize activity, clear, and conversion transitions without timestamp ordering. Conversion can establish the fence before its matching activity projection, while activity from a newer Cart revision remains authoritative. **Watch for:** no confirmed High/Medium Task 36 behavioral defects remain after the conversion-before-projection remediation.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

Each authenticated cart write advances `activityRevision` in its atomic Cart update. Projection writes replace only lower revisions, preventing delayed hydration or scheduling from letting stale activity overwrite or reopen a newer episode.

Exact checkout compare-and-clear carries the pre-clear revision into a post-commit `CONVERTED` projection built from immutable Order data. Conversion outranks `OPEN` at the same revision, can create the owner fence when activity has not arrived, and cannot touch a newer revision.

The projection remains failure-isolated and non-authoritative. It stores bounded merchandise data without contact or consent fields, expires after thirty days, and adds no reminder sender, detector, route, or customer/admin surface.

## Owner-unique CAS closes both ordering races

`replaceIfNewer` accepts only a lower stored revision, and the duplicate-key fallback re-evaluates that same predicate after a concurrent creator wins. Conversion extends the predicate only for `OPEN` at the exact cleared revision, so it can establish `CONVERTED` before activity arrives without allowing same-revision activity to reopen it. A post-checkout mutation has a greater Cart revision and survives the older conversion attempt; `lastActivityAt` remains metadata rather than an ordering authority.

The conversion snapshot uses persisted Order item identities, quantities, unit prices, and merchandise subtotal, plus the captured Cart `updatedAt`. It therefore does not depend on a post-commit catalogue read or millisecond equality to identify the converted episode.

## Best-effort capture stays outside commerce and messaging authority

Projection failures are swallowed after bounded logging, so they cannot roll back Cart or Order truth. Owner uniqueness, state/activity lookup, and TTL indexes are registered with the deployment index synchronizer; the schema excludes contact, address, consent, coupon, device, and message data. No Task 36 path publishes notifications or classifies an episode as abandoned without a future approved inactivity policy.

The reported database smoke checks cover lower-revision rejection, conversion-before-activity, and newer-cart preservation.
